# Atlas AI ERP

> An intelligent ERP platform designed to automate business operations through Artificial Intelligence.

## About

Atlas AI ERP is an ongoing project focused on building a modern Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system powered by Artificial Intelligence.

The goal is to replace traditional spreadsheets with a smarter, centralized platform capable of automating business processes, organizing data, and providing intelligent insights to support decision-making.

This project also represents my journey in software development, artificial intelligence, and business automation.

## Features (Planned)

* 🤖 AI-powered Assistant
* 💰 Financial Management
* 📦 Inventory Control
* 👥 Employee Management
* 📊 Business Analytics
* 🔄 Workflow Automation
* 📄 Smart Reports

## Project Status

🚧 Currently under development.

New features, documentation, and improvements will be added as the project evolves.

## Vision

I believe business management should be simple, intelligent, and accessible.

Atlas AI ERP aims to help companies reduce repetitive work, improve productivity, and make better decisions through automation and Artificial Intelligence.
